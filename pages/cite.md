<h1 class="cow-title">How to cite</h1>
<p class="cow-lead">The website, a taxon page, the data and the images: what to write in your references.</p>
</div>
</plate-strip>

<div class="cow-doc">

<div class="cow-doc__card">
<p class="cow-doc__kicker">The website</p>
<p class="cow-doc__cite">Mikó, I. &amp; Tilton, S. (2026) <i>Ceraphronoidea of the World</i>. Available from https://shanetiltongc.github.io/Ceraphronoidea-of-the-World/ (accessed [date]).</p>
</div>

<div class="cow-doc__card">
<p class="cow-doc__kicker">A taxon page</p>
<p>Every taxon page has a <b>Cite this page</b> box on its Overview, with a ready citation and BibTeX. For example:</p>
<p class="cow-doc__cite"><i>Dendrocerus carpenteri</i> (Curtis, 1829). In: <i>Ceraphronoidea of the World</i>. Available from https://shanetiltongc.github.io/Ceraphronoidea-of-the-World/#/otus/1372849/overview (accessed [date]).</p>
</div>

<div class="cow-doc__card">
<p class="cow-doc__kicker">Nomenclature</p>
<p>Names follow Johnson, N.F. &amp; Musetti, L. (2004) Catalog of the systematic literature of the superfamily Ceraphronoidea (Hymenoptera). <i>Contributions of the American Entomological Institute</i>, 33, 1–149. Cite the original descriptions too: each taxon page lists them under <b>Nomenclature → References</b>.</p>
</div>

<div class="cow-doc__card">
<p class="cow-doc__kicker">Data</p>
<p>Lists on this site (host records, types, distributions, literature) can be downloaded as CSV from each page. Cite the website as above and give the date you downloaded the data. Versioned data releases with a DOI on Zenodo are planned; this page will give their DOIs.</p>
</div>

<div class="cow-doc__card">
<p class="cow-doc__kicker">Images</p>
<p>Images are open access under the CC BY-NC-SA licence unless stated otherwise. Credit the photographer (most specimen photographs are by István Mikó) and this website.</p>
</div>
